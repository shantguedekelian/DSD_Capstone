Ideas this points to (to test, not proven)

1

Send recheck instructions once, outside the comments

Recheck and response text is about 8% of all comments.

2

Turn repeated requests into an applicant checklist

For example: water heater details, who pays water and sewer fees, earthwork tables.

3

Use one standard wording for near-duplicates

For example, “Indicate project construction cost” shows up in many slightly different forms.

Limits and next steps

Keep in mind

  • Partial data: 2 of 33 files, so numbers will shift
  • Raw, unvalidated DSD data: preliminary, not official findings
  • We can't tell which comments were fixed: the “resolved” flag is unreliable
  • Groups are approximate: wording and typos vary
